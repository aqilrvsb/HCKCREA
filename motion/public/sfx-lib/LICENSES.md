# PeningLab Motion SFX library: licenses

Every file in this folder comes from a **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)** source: Kenney audio packs (kenney.nl) and individual Freesound uploads whose sound page lists the license as "Creative Commons 0". No attribution is legally required. Credits are listed anyway. License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Sound pages were checked on 2026-10-12. Freesound uploads that are remixes of non-CC0 sounds, or that say they derive from another user's sound of unknown license, were excluded. Kenney packs ship a License.txt stating CC0.

Processing (PeningLab): leading silence trimmed (<=10 ms), trailing silence trimmed, 15 ms fade-out (longer fades only on capped files), length capped, gain set to about -16 LUFS integrated or limited to -1 dBTP true peak (peak-normalised when too short for LUFS), encoded 44.1 kHz stereo MP3 at 192 kbps. Some files take one region of a longer source (a single stroke, scratch or roll: underline_1-3, cash_2, drumroll_2, scratch_2) and some get a high-pass filter to remove mic rumble or wind blast.
